FAQ

Questions we’re tired of answering.

Is Codify production-ready? +
Define “ready.” The software works exactly as intended. It is your definition of “intended” that we cannot be held responsible for.
Does Codify have a staging environment? +
We believe staging environments breed complacency. True confidence comes from deploying directly to production and watching your error rates in real time. It’s like extreme sports, but for your career.
What happens when something goes wrong? +
We have a robust “It wasn’t us” policy. Our Blame Dashboard will identify the exact commit, the exact author, and auto-draft a Slack message for you. We also provide a template postmortem that blames infrastructure.
Does Codify support TypeScript? +
Technically yes, but our AI has strong opinions. It will compile your TypeScript and then silently suggest you rewrite it in JavaScript “for performance.” We’re working on this. We are not working on this.
How is your uptime 99%? +
Great question. We define “up” as “the server is physically on.” Occasional periods of returning HTTP 500 still count as uptime under our innovative “the server tried its best” SLA.
